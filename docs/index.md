# Why Baobab?

Across Africa and emerging markets, organisations face increasing pressure to modernise services, improve operational efficiency, strengthen governance and embrace artificial intelligence. Yet many existing enterprise platforms remain expensive, fragmented, vendor-centric or difficult to adapt to local realities.

Baobab was created to address these challenges.

Our goal is to provide an open, modern and intelligent enterprise platform that empowers organisations to build digital capabilities with confidence. By combining enterprise architecture, cloud-native technologies and AI-driven automation, Baobab enables organisations to focus on delivering value rather than managing complexity.

Whether supporting public sector transformation, commercial enterprises, educational institutions or development programmes, Baobab provides a trusted foundation for sustainable digital innovation.

> **Our mission is to build Africa's most trusted enterprise platform for intelligent business operations.**
